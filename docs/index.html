¶ Pilcrow — Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Pilcrow has no server, no account and no analytics. The text you write is passed to Chrome's built-in on-device AI model and discarded. It is never uploaded, never logged, and never seen by the developer or anyone else.

The text you write

When Pilcrow checks a text field, the contents of that field are passed to Chrome's built-in AI APIs — the Prompt, Proofreader, Rewriter, Writer, Summarizer, Translator and Language Detector models that run inside your browser, on your own machine.

No network request is made. Pilcrow works with the network switched off. Nothing is written to disk, and nothing is retained after the suggestions are produced.

What is stored, and where

Pilcrow keeps your own configuration in Chrome's extension storage:

DataStorageLeaves your device?
Settings — categories, writing goals, house-style rules, quiet-site list, languages chrome.storage.sync Only through Chrome Sync, if you have it on
Personal dictionary chrome.storage.local No
Snippets chrome.storage.local No

One honest caveat about settings. Settings are stored in chrome.storage.sync so they follow you between computers. If you are signed into Chrome with sync enabled, Chrome — not Pilcrow — copies them through your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. This affects your settings only, including any house-style rules you write. It never affects the text you are checking, your dictionary or your snippets, which stay on the device.

What Pilcrow never does

  • No accounts, sign-in or user identifiers of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, and no data sold, rented or shared with anybody.
  • No remote code — every line that runs is bundled in the extension package.
  • No third-party services, SDKs or trackers.
  • No reading or transmitting of page content beyond the text field you are working in.

Permissions, and why each one exists

storageSaves your settings, dictionary and snippets, as described above.
offscreenChrome's AI APIs do not exist in a service worker. Pilcrow hosts them in an offscreen document, which is the only context where they are available.
sidePanelShows suggestions and tools in Chrome's side panel.
contextMenusAdds the right-click actions for selected text.
activeTab and scriptingLets the popup and the diagnostics page reach the tab you are looking at.
<all_urls> A writing assistant has to work in whatever text field you are typing in, and there is no fixed list of sites where people write. Access is used solely to read and edit the focused text field. You can switch Pilcrow off for any site from the popup, or list sites to stay quiet on in Settings.

Model downloads

The first check downloads Chrome's on-device model. That download is performed by Chrome itself, from Google, and is governed by Google's terms — Pilcrow only asks Chrome whether the model is ready and, if you press the button, to fetch it. Once it is on your machine, no further network access is needed.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Reset everything erases every setting, dictionary entry and snippet. Uninstalling the extension removes all of it as well. Because there is no server, there is nothing else to delete and no request to make of anyone.

Children

Pilcrow collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it, and the previous wording stays in the repository's history.
